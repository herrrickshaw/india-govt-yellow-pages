- 'Contact' = signed the instrument's paperwork; the signer of a notification is usually the Joint/Deputy/Under Secretary of the administering division, which is the right door to knock on, but not an officially published helpdesk.
- Instrument names discovered from titles are heuristic (Scheme / Yojana / Mission / Fund / PLI / Guidelines for …); near-duplicates are possible.
- `moved_since_signing = yes` comes from transfer news / ER sheets and is a lead to verify.
